Chandler S. White, IV

Geospatial information science professional.

Hello! I'm a GIS professional with over 15 years of experience in the public and private sectors. I currently serve as GIS Technical Coordinator with the Department of Information Technology at Clayton County, Georgia.

My professional interests lie in spatial analysis, research, and application development; my skills range from enterprise system design to field data collection. I'm passionate about applying geospatial technology to real-world problems and supporting data-driven decision making.

What I do

  • Spatial analysis and modeling — R, ArcGIS, and specialized modeling software.
  • Enterprise GIS — system design, database administration, and application development for government operations.
  • Research and analysis — quantitative methods in statistical modeling and scientific research, academic and applied.

Credentials

  • M.S. Geospatial Science — University of North Alabama (2017)
  • Esri Special Achievement in GIS Award (2025)
